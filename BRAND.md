## The game in one line
**Balloon Bonkers: Keep It Up!** A Roblox party game: keep the balloon in the air,
hit it so the *next* player misses, and shout GOTCHA when they do.

**Play link:** https://www.roblox.com/games/101666093048466/Balloon-Bonkers

## What makes it fun (post ideas come from here)
- **GOTCHA:** drop shots and smashes that make the next player miss.
- **Skill moves:** dive saves, charged hits, whackers (18 to unlock).
- **Cartoon chaos:** the whale, the shark, the robo-vacuum, the cat paw and other hazards.
- **Knocked out isn't boring:** you join the Balcony Bandits and throw stuff at the players still in.
- **12 crazy places** to play in.
- **Collect them all:** balloon skins and the Balloon Road progression.
- **Events:** Spooky Night runs until Monday night, 2 November 2026 (ends 3 Nov 00:00 UTC).

## Who we talk to
Roblox players and their friends. Instagram and Facebook users are 13+, and many
parents follow too, so every post must be fine for a family to see.

## Voice
- Short, loud, fun. One idea per post. Emojis are welcome (🎈💥😂).
- English, so players anywhere can read it.
- Talk like a player, not like an ad: challenges, questions, "who's your GOTCHA victim?"
- Always end with a reason to play or reply: a question, a challenge, or "play free on Roblox".

## Rules (never break these)
- **Show the real game.** Gameplay images must be real screenshots or the official art.
  AI-made images are for mood and promo only and must never pretend to be gameplay.
- **No pressure to spend.** Never push Robux purchases, no "buy now before it's gone"
  aimed at kids, no fake countdowns. Mentioning that an item exists is fine.
- **No scams or bait:** never "free Robux", giveaways we can't run, or fake numbers.
- **No other brands:** no Nintendo, Disney or other characters, logos or music.
- **No real people** in images without their permission, and never children's faces.
- Keep it honest: don't claim player counts, rankings or awards we don't have.

## Post mix (rotate through these)
1. **GOTCHA moment:** a funny screenshot plus a caption that invites comments.
2. **Meet the chaos:** one hazard at a time (the whale, the shark, the cat paw...).
3. **Map spotlight:** one of the 12 places.
4. **Collect them all:** a balloon skin or whacker.
5. **Challenge / question:** "Can you dive-save a smash? 👇"
6. **Event or update news:** Spooky Night, new maps, new balloons.
